Our History & Evolution

ABOUT US | OUR JOURNEY (2009-2026)

How a dedicated welfare society transformed into Hooghly's premier tertiary eye hospital.

01

From Camps to Care (2009)

In 2009, the journey began not as a hospital, but as the registered Bhadreswar Netradarpan Welfare Foundation with a simple humanitarian dream — to remove darkness from the lives of marginalized communities suffering from avoidable blindness across Hooghly and neighboring districts.

02

From Service to Super-Specialty Hospital

What started as weekend community eye camps gradually evolved into a permanent, world-class medical institute. Netradarpan Hospital was established at Bhadreswar with laminar airflow surgical suites, advanced Phaco cataract systems, and primary eye care clinics.

03

2026 — Universal Vision For All

Today in 2026, Netradarpan Hospital stands as a beacon of affordable eye care. Empanelled with Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY and Swasthya Sathi, we ensure that zero patients are turned away due to lack of financial resources.

Our Flagship Initiative

HOSPITAL IN A BAG

Taking the Hospital to the Last Mile — Bringing Comprehensive Eye Care Closer to Every Doorstep.

Flagship Priority Programme

Bringing Eye Care to the Last Mile

“If people cannot reach the hospital, the hospital must reach the people.”

Hospital in a Bag is a flagship community eye-care initiative of Netradarpan Hospital, designed to take essential eye-screening facilities, precision diagnostic equipment, and trained medical teams directly to underserved rural communities. Our aim is to reach the last village, the last home, and the last person who may otherwise be unable to access hospital-based eye care.

Non-Mydriatic Fundus

Captures ultra-clear eye scans without pupil dilation to detect early ocular conditions and glaucoma.

Handheld Auto-Refractor

Instant digital refractive calculation in under 30 seconds for precise, on-the-spot spectacle prescriptions.

Rebound Tonometer

Gentle intraocular pressure (IOP) measurement for swift, anesthesia-free glaucoma screening in the field.

5G Cloud Tele-Link

Encrypted tele-ophthalmology link connecting grassroots optometrists directly with senior eye surgeons at Bhadreswar.

How the "Hospital in a Bag" Doorstep Model Operates

1

Doorstep Field Screening

Paramedics visit rural households, village clubs, and elderly homes with portable diagnostic gear.

2

AI Pre-Triage & Cloud Sync

Diagnostic scans are synced instantly to cloud servers with automated AI assessment for rapid risk classification.

3

Doctor Tele-Consultation

Senior ophthalmologists at the base hospital review images remotely and recommend treatment or glasses.

4

Hospital Fast-Track

Patients diagnosed with cataracts or complex ocular issues are transported to Bhadreswar for 100% cashless treatment.
