Our Eye Care Specialities

Anterior Segment

Cataract & Phaco Surgery

Painless, stitchless, micro-incision cataract surgery (MICS) with advanced Foldable, Toric, and Multifocal Intraocular Lens (IOL) implants.

  • Day Care Procedure (No Admission Required)
  • Premium Monofocal & Multifocal Lenses
  • Ayushman Bharat & Swasthya Sathi Covered
Optometry Unit

Primary Eye Check-up & Refraction

Senior Optometrist led practice offering primary eye examinations, precise computerized refraction, binocular vision assessment, and contact lens fitting & training.

  • Computerized Refraction & Vision Testing
  • Contact Lens Assessment & Training
  • Primary Eye Screening & Consultation
Glaucoma Clinic

Glaucoma & Laser Clinic

The "Silent Thief of Sight" caught early through Goldman Applanation Tonometry, computerized visual field perimetry, and laser iridotomy.

  • Computerized Visual Field Analysis
  • Nd:YAG Laser Peripheral Iridotomy
  • Medical & Surgical IOP Management
