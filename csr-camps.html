Community Outreach

EYE SCREENING CAMPS

Rural & Village Camps

Free comprehensive eye checkups held in panchayat halls, rural clubs, and temples. Includes vision refraction, cataract detection, and eye drop distribution.

School Sight Screenings

Dedicated screening drives in government and primary schools to identify refractive errors, amblyopia (lazy eye), and squint in young children early.

Workplace & Community Drives

Collaborative health initiatives with industrial units, jute mills, banks, and NGOs to screen factory workers, drivers, and daily wage earners.
