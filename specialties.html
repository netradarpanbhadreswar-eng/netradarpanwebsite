Clinical Eye Care Specialties

Comprehensive ophthalmic care backed by high-precision surgical suites, lasers, and computerized diagnostic units.

Home / Clinical Specialties
Ophthalmic Departments

Specialized Clinical Services

Each wing is led by senior ophthalmologists and fellowship-trained eye surgeons using evidence-based clinical protocols.

Anterior Segment

Cataract & Phaco Surgery

Micro-Incision Cataract Surgery (MICS) performed using ultrasonic phacoemulsification through a 2.2mm micro-incision with zero injections or stitches.

  • Hydrophobic Foldable, Toric & Multifocal IOLs
  • Optical Biometry & Non-Contact Keratometry
  • Post-op rapid visual recovery within 24 hours
  • Ayushman Bharat & Swasthya Sathi Empanelled
Optometry Unit

Primary Eye Check-up & Refraction

Dedicated practice for visual acuity evaluation, precise refraction, contact lens assessment & training, and binocular vision care led by Senior Optometrist.

  • Precise Refraction & Spectacle Prescription
  • Contact Lens Assessment & Training
  • Primary Eye Check-up & Screenings
Glaucoma Clinic

Glaucoma & Laser Clinic

Early identification of high intraocular pressure to prevent optic nerve damage through Humphrey visual field perimetry, gonioscopy, and Nd:YAG laser iridotomy.

  • Goldmann Applanation Tonometry (GAT)
  • Computerized Humphrey Visual Field Analyzer
  • Nd:YAG Laser Peripheral Iridotomy (LPI)
  • Medical & Trabeculectomy Surgical Care
Cornea Unit

Cornea & Ocular Surface

Expert diagnostic evaluation and treatment of corneal ulcers, infections, chemical burns, pterygium excision with conjunctival autograft, and dry eye syndrome.

  • Keratoconus Evaluation & Corneal Topography
  • Pterygium Excision with Fibrin Glue Graft
  • Advanced Dry Eye Management & Punctal Plugs
  • Therapeutic & Optical Keratoplasty Evaluation
Pediatric Care

Pediatric Ophthalmology & Squint

Dedicated child-friendly eye examinations for newborns and school-age children suffering from refractive errors, lazy eye (amblyopia), squint, and congenital eye disorders.

  • Cycloplegic Refraction for Toddlers & Children
  • Amblyopia (Lazy Eye) Occlusion Therapy
  • Surgical Correction of Strabismus (Squint)
  • Congenital Nasolacrimal Duct Obstruction Syringing
Optical Clinic

Refraction & Optical Clinic

Computerized auto-refractometry and subjective refraction by certified optometrists, followed by custom corrective lens dispensing and low vision aids.

  • Precision Computerized Eye Testing
  • Progressive & Anti-Glare Digital Lenses
  • Contact Lens Fitting & Counseling
  • Low Vision Rehabilitation Devices
